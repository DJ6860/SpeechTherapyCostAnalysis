Cost Analysis: Why Speech Therapy Isn't Accessible

I researched the barriers keeping 90-95% of people who stutter from getting professional help. This analysis documents what I found: cost is the primary barrier. Traditional speech therapy costs $6,500 per year. Insurance covers only 35% on average. Explore the data below to understand the gap, and visit Fluent and Speak Up to see how I'm addressing it.

Cost Comparison: Traditional vs. Digital Solutions

The price difference is stark. Traditional speech therapy costs thousands. Apps cost $150-180 per year. Fluent costs zero. Here's how they compare.

Insurance Coverage: The Access Gap

Even with insurance, most people aren't covered. Only about 30% have full coverage for speech therapy. The other 70% either have no coverage or partial coverage, meaning they pay most or all of the cost themselves.

Access Barriers: Why 90% Go Untreated

Cost is the #1 barrier, but it's not the only one. Geographic access, scheduling, and stigma also keep people from getting help. This chart shows the barriers teens and families face.

Costs Across America: Geographic Variation

Cost varies dramatically by region. A speech therapy session in Texas might cost $100, but in New York it costs $175. This geographic variation means where you live determines what you'll pay.

Market Reality: The Untreated Population

The numbers tell the story. 70 million people stutter globally. 3 million in the United States. But only 5-10% get professional treatment. That means 2.7-2.85 million US teens go without help, largely due to cost.

Methodology

This research synthesizes data from the National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders (NIDCD), U.S. Census Bureau, industry cost aggregators, and regional therapy provider estimates. I analyzed therapy costs, insurance reimbursement rates, app pricing, and market size to understand the barriers to speech therapy access. All sources are cited in the full research summary.
